# Catholic Theological Institute, Port Moresby, Papua New Guinea — Philosophy of Science and Nature

- **출처**: [syllabus_pdf](https://www.philsci.org/docs/10.Miller_Syllabus_2020_PH_104_Philosophy_of_Science_and_Nature.pdf) ✔fetch 확인
- 담당 Ryan Miller, Ph.L. · Second Term, 2020 · Philosophy (Certificate/Diploma in Philosophy & Philosophical Studies; Christian Studies) · First-year (100-level), required 3-credit undergraduate course · 과목코드 PH 104

**NOS 초점**: 이 강의는 아리스토텔레스·토미즘 자연철학을 배경으로 근대·현대 과학철학의 핵심 NOS 쟁점을 다룬다. "과학이란 무엇인가" 파트에서 Popper의 반증가능성과 구획(demarcation) 문제, Kuhn의 패러다임과 과학혁명, Lonergan의 경험적 방법의 규범(canons)을 통해 과학의 방법론과 발전 방식을 비교·검토한다. 나아가 과학적 이상화(idealization)와 모델·현상의 관계, 빅뱅 우주론에서의 창조·시간, 인과성과 목적론(teleology)을 다루며, 과학적 진보가 철학에, 철학적 비판이 과학에 각각 제기하는 도전을 상호적으로 성찰한다. 실재론·사회적 성격보다는 방법론(반증·패러다임·경험적 방법)과 인과·목적론에 초점이 있다.

**주요 주제**: Aristotelian/Thomistic philosophy of nature: principles, substance/accident, matter/form, five causes, primary/secondary causality; Philosophy of science: falsification, paradigms, and the canons of empirical method; Creation, place, and space in big bang cosmology; Models and phenomena in modern physics; Generation, corruption, and atomism in ancient and modern chemistry; Five causes case study: genetics; Causality and teleology in evolution

**교재**: Verschuuren, Gerard M. Aquinas and Modern Science: A New Synthesis of Faith and Reason. Kettering: Angelico Press, 2016.

**평가**: Task 1: Daily Reading Quiz [1% each, totalling 28%] — short-answer question each class on assigned reading. Task 2: In-Class Written Exam on Aristotle's Principles of Nature (matter/form, substance/accident, causality) [16%], 30 min, assigned Mon 1 June. Task 3: Short Response Paper "Conceptions of Science" — 300 words contrasting two of Aristotle/Popper/Kuhn/Lonergan [10%], due Wed 24 June (rubric 25/25/25/25). Task 4: Response Paper "Modern Science" — 500 words on how modern science and philosophy challenge each other in physics or chemistry [16%], due Wed 22 July (rubric 20/20/30/30). Task 5: Written Final Exam "Causality in Modern Science" — five causes operative in genetics [30%], per examination timetable. (Total assessed work ~3000 words.)
