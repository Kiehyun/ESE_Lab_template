# English — Official Submission Guidelines

## Instructions for Authors

### Language
To be considered for review, submissions should be sufficiently proofread, internationalised and engage with prior research published in RISE, if English is not your first language.

### Editorial procedure — Double-blind peer review
This journal follows a double-blind reviewing procedure. The author remains anonymous to the reviewers throughout peer review. It is the author's responsibility to anonymize the manuscript and any associated materials:
- Author names, affiliations and any other potentially identifying information should be removed from the manuscript text and any accompanying files (such as figures or supplementary material).
- A separate Title Page should be submitted, containing title, author names, affiliations, and the contact information of the corresponding author. Any acknowledgements, disclosures, or funding information should also be included on this page.
- Authors should avoid citing their own work in a way that could reveal their identity.

### Length of Article
Authors are encouraged to submit papers to a maximum length of **8000 words including references** (shorter contributions are welcome). In rare cases where the authors make a persuasive case that a work makes a highly significant original contribution to knowledge in science education, the authors may contact the editors ahead of time, who may choose to publish long works.

### Manuscript Submission
Submission of a manuscript implies: that the work described has not been published before; that it is not under consideration for publication anywhere else; that its publication has been approved by all co-authors, if any, as well as by the responsible authorities — tacitly or explicitly — at the institute where the work has been carried out. The publisher will not be held legally responsible should there be any claims for compensation.

**Permissions.** Authors wishing to include figures, tables, or text passages already published elsewhere must obtain permission from the copyright owner(s) for both print and online formats and include evidence of permission when submitting. Material received without such evidence is assumed to originate from the authors.

**Online Submission.** Follow the "Submit manuscript" hyperlink and upload all files following the on-screen instructions.

**Source Files.** Provide all relevant editable source files at every submission and revision. Failing to submit a complete set of editable source files results in the article not being considered for review. Submit manuscript text in common word-processing formats such as **.docx or LaTeX**.

### Peer Review Process
There are two phases to a RISE review.

**Phase 1.** A Co-Editor in Chief conducts an initial review, considering: word limits; structure; quality of research approach; quality of writing; English proficiency; and potential contribution to science education research. This results in one of three decisions: (1) reject without review; (2) send for peer review (Phase 2); or (3) the opportunity to revise before review (e.g., reduce word count). Initial review usually takes about a week.

**Phase 2.** Managed by a co-editor or Associate Editor; two reviewers from the RISE pool are assigned. RISE uses a double-blind process. If suitable for peer review, authors can expect notification of the outcome **within 3 months** of submission if all goes to plan. In most cases revisions are required, often over additional rounds. When responding to revisions, the editorial team requires: (1) a document (e.g., table/list) outlining how reviewers' feedback has been addressed (or not) with justification; and (2) clearly shown changes/additions (e.g., highlighted).

**The five review standards:** (1) Minor revisions; (2) Revisions; (3) Major revisions; (4) Reject and resubmit; (5) Reject.

**Becoming a reviewer.** A RISE reviewer usually holds a higher degree (or is a late-stage PhD candidate with a supervisor's reference), is research active in science education or related areas, and is experienced with peer review. Reviewers normally accept/decline within one week and complete reviews within four weeks. Express interest to the Co-Editors in Chief (Kok-Sing Tang and James Davis, info@asera.org.au).

### Title Page
Include the following:
- **Title** — concise and informative.
- **Author information** — name(s); affiliation(s) (institution, department, city, state, country); a clear indication and active e-mail of the corresponding author; the 16-digit ORCID of the author(s) if available.
- **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, such as ChatGPT, do not currently satisfy authorship criteria (authorship carries accountability that cannot be applied to LLMs). **Use of an LLM should be properly documented in the Methods section** (or a suitable alternative part). Use of an LLM for "AI-assisted copy editing" (readability/style/grammar/spelling/punctuation/tone improvements to human-generated text, without generative editorial work or autonomous content creation) need **not** be declared; human accountability for the final text is required.
- **Abstract** — provide an abstract of **150 to 250 words**, with no undefined abbreviations or unspecified references.
- **Keywords** — provide **4 to 6 keywords** for indexing.

**Statements and Declarations.** The following statements should be included under the heading **"Statements and Declarations"** for inclusion in the published paper. *Submissions that do not include relevant declarations will be returned as incomplete.*
- **Competing Interests:** authors must disclose financial or non-financial interests directly or indirectly related to the submitted work (see "Competing Interests" below).

### Text
**Text Formatting.** Manuscripts should be submitted in Word. Use a normal, plain font (e.g., 10-point Times Roman); italics for emphasis; automatic page numbering; no field functions; tab stops (not the space bar) for indents; the table function (not spreadsheets) for tables; the equation editor or MathType for equations; save as .docx (Word 2007+) or .doc. Math-heavy manuscripts may be submitted in LaTeX (Springer Nature LaTeX template recommended).

**Headings.** Use no more than three levels of displayed headings.

**Abbreviations.** Define at first mention and use consistently thereafter.

**Footnotes.** May give additional information including a reference citation, but should not consist solely of a reference citation and should never include bibliographic details; no figures/tables in footnotes. Text footnotes are numbered consecutively; table footnotes use superscript lowercase letters (or asterisks for significance values). Footnotes to title/authors carry no reference symbols. Always use footnotes, not endnotes.

**Acknowledgments.** Place acknowledgments of people, grants, funds, etc. in a separate section **on the title page**. Write funding organizations' names in full.

### References
**Citation.** Cite references in the text by name and year in parentheses. Examples: *Negotiation research spans many disciplines (Thompson, 1990). This result was later contradicted by Becker and Seligman (1996). This effect has been widely studied (Abbott, 1991; Barakat et al., 1995; Kelso & Smith, 1998; Medvec et al., 1999).* Follow **official APA version 7** on the number of authors in reference list entries (include all authors up to 20; for larger groups, first 19 names + ellipsis + final author). If authors shorten with "et al.", this is retained.

**Reference list.** Include only works cited in the text and published or accepted for publication (personal communications and unpublished works only in text). Alphabetize by first author's last name. Italicize journal names and book titles. Always include **DOIs as full DOI links** (e.g., "https://doi.org/abc"). APA-7 examples:
- *Journal article:* Grady, J. S., Her, M., Moreno, G., Perez, C., & Yelinek, J. (2019). Emotions in storybooks… *Psychology of Popular Media Culture, 8*(3), 207–217. https://doi.org/10.1037/ppm0000185
- *Book:* Sapolsky, R. M. (2017). *Behave: The biology of humans at our best and worst.* Penguin Books.
- *Book chapter:* Dillard, J. P. (2020). Currents in the study of persuasion. In M. B. Oliver, A. A. Raney, & J. Bryant (Eds.), *Media effects: Advances in theory and research* (4th ed., pp. 115–129). Routledge.
- *Online document:* Fagan, J. (2019, March 25). *Nursing clinical brain.* OER Commons. Retrieved January 7, 2020, from https://www.oercommons.org/…

### Bibliographic information with non-English titles
Non-English titles must be translated, shown in parentheses after the original. Example: *Raae, P. H. (2012). Den nordiske uddannelsesmodel og det danske gymnasium (The Nordic educational model and the Danish upper secondary school). Nordic Studies in Education, 32(03–04), 311–320.*

### Tables
Number all tables with Arabic numerals; cite in text in consecutive order; supply a caption (title) explaining the components. Identify previously published material by a reference at the end of the caption. Table footnotes use superscript lowercase letters (or asterisks for significance values), beneath the table body.

### Artwork and Illustrations Guidelines
- **Electronic submission:** supply all figures electronically; indicate the graphics program. Preferred formats: **EPS** (vector), **TIFF** (halftone); MS Office files acceptable. Embed fonts in vector graphics. Name files `Fig` + number (e.g., `Fig1.eps`).
- **Line Art:** black-and-white, no shading; lines ≥ **0.1 mm (0.3 pt)**; scanned/bitmap line drawings ≥ **1200 dpi**.
- **Halftone Art:** photographs/drawings with fine shading; ≥ **300 dpi**; use scale bars for magnification.
- **Combination Art:** halftone + line art; ≥ **600 dpi**.
- **Color Art:** free for online; if printed B/W, ensure main information is still visible and don't refer to color in captions; submit as RGB (8 bits/channel).
- **Figure Lettering:** Helvetica/Arial; ~2–3 mm (8–12 pt), consistent sizing; avoid shading/outline letters; no titles/captions inside illustrations.
- **Figure Numbering:** Arabic numerals; cite in consecutive order; parts as (a), (b), (c); appendix figures continue main numbering; online-appendix (SI) figures numbered separately.
- **Figure Captions:** concise; in the text file (not the figure file); begin with **Fig.** in bold + number in bold; no punctuation after the number or at caption end; identify previously published material with a reference at caption end.
- **Placement and Size:** within the body of the text; size to column width; for large-sized journals 84 mm (double-column) or 174 mm (single-column) wide, ≤ 234 mm high; for small-sized journals 119 mm wide, ≤ 195 mm high.
- **Accessibility:** descriptive captions; patterns in addition to colors; lettering contrast ≥ 4.5:1.
- **Generative AI Images:** adhere to Springer's policy on generative-AI images.

### Supplementary Information (SI)
Electronic multimedia and other supplementary files may be published online with the article. Read the journal's Research data policy before submitting datasets; archive research data in repositories where possible. Refer to supplementary files as "Online Resource" (e.g., Online Resource 3) and name them consecutively (e.g., `ESM_3.mpg`). Provide a concise caption for each. SI is published as received (no editing). **SI files are sent to peer reviewers — they must not contain author names, affiliations, or any potentially identifying information.**

### Editing Services
Springer Nature Author Services (and a free Grammar Check tool) can assist with English editing, formatting, figures, translation, etc. Using these services is not a requirement for publication and does not guarantee acceptance.

## Ethical Responsibilities of Authors
The journal upholds research integrity and, as a COPE member, follows COPE guidelines on misconduct. Good scientific practice includes: no simultaneous submission to more than one journal; original, not previously published work (unless an expansion, with transparency to avoid text-recycling/self-plagiarism); no salami-slicing; clear, honest presentation without fabrication, falsification, or inappropriate data/image manipulation; no plagiarism (proper acknowledgement, quotation marks for verbatim, permissions for copyrighted material). The journal may screen for plagiarism. Authors must hold permissions for software, questionnaires/surveys, and scales used. Research and non-research articles must cite appropriate, relevant literature; **excessive and inappropriate self-citation (or coordinated collective self-citation) is strongly discouraged.** Avoid untrue statements/personal attacks; flag dual-use research. Ensure the author group, corresponding author, and author order are correct at submission; **authorship changes cannot be made after acceptance.** On request, authors should provide documentation/data to verify results. Suspected misconduct is investigated per COPE, potentially leading to rejection, erratum/correction, expression of concern, or retraction.

**Fundamental errors.** Authors must correct significant errors in published work (correction or retraction).

**Suggesting/excluding reviewers.** Authors may suggest independent reviewers (institutional e-mail or other identity verification required) or request exclusions; the journal may not use suggestions.

## Authorship principles
All authors must: (1) make substantial contributions to conception/design or acquisition/analysis/interpretation of data or new software; (2) draft or critically revise the work; (3) approve the version to be published; and (4) agree to be accountable for all aspects (based on ICMJE).

**Disclosures and declarations.** All authors must include information on sources of funding; financial or non-financial interests; study-specific ethics-committee approval for research involving humans and/or animals; informed consent if human participants were involved; and an animal-welfare statement if applicable.

**Data transparency.** Ensure all data, materials, software/code support the published claims and comply with field standards.

**Role of the Corresponding Author.** Ensures all listed authors approved the manuscript (names and order); manages all journal–co-author communication before and after publication; provides transparency on re-use/unpublished material in a cover letter; ensures disclosures, declarations, and data statements are included.

**Author contributions.** Where possible, include contribution statements specifying each author's contribution **on the separate title page** (free text or CRediT taxonomy). For articles based primarily on a student's dissertation/thesis, the student is usually listed as principal author.

**Affiliation.** Primary affiliation = institution where the majority of the work was done; current address may additionally be stated. Not updated after publication.

**Changes to authorship.** Ensure correct author group/order at submission; changes (add/delete authors, corresponding author, order) are **not accepted after acceptance**. Author names are published exactly as on the accepted submission.

**Author identification.** Authors are recommended to use an ORCID ID.

**Deceased/incapacitated authors; authorship disputes; confidentiality** — handled per the stated procedures; authors treat all journal communication as confidential.

## Compliance with Ethical Standards
To ensure objectivity and transparency, authors should include information on sources of funding, potential conflicts of interest (financial or non-financial), informed consent (if human participants), and an animal-welfare statement (if animals). When submitting, include applicable statements in a separate section entitled **"Compliance with Ethical Standards"**: disclosure of potential conflicts of interest; research involving human participants and/or animals; informed consent. Standards may vary slightly per journal (single vs double blind). The corresponding author should be prepared to collect compliance documentation. The Editors may reject manuscripts not complying with these guidelines.

## Competing Interests
Authors must disclose interests directly or indirectly related to the submitted work, covering the last **3 years** (and earlier if reasonably perceived as influencing the work). Interests to disclose include: **Funding** (research grants — give funder and grant number — and research support); **Employment**; **Financial interests** (stocks/shares, consultation fees, patents); **Non-financial interests** (editorial/advisory board positions, consulting for educational purposes, expert witness, mentoring, etc.). Primary research and review articles require a disclosure statement.

**Summary of requirements.** Summarize the above in a statement on a **title page separate from the manuscript**, in a section entitled **"Declarations"**. Declarations include **Funding, Competing interests, Ethics approval, Consent, Data/Materials/Code availability, and Authors' contribution statements.** Example wordings:
- *Funding (none):* "The authors did not receive support from any organization for the submitted work." / "No funding was received for conducting this study."
- *No competing interests:* "The authors have no relevant financial or non-financial interests to disclose." / "The authors have no competing interests to declare that are relevant to the content of this article."

The Editor-in-Chief may reject submissions not meeting these requirements.

## After acceptance
On acceptance, the article goes to Production for typesetting; you receive e-mails to confirm affiliation, choose publishing model, arrange rights/payment, and access proofs.
- **Article publishing agreement:** exclusive licence to publish or copyright transfer (per journal policy).
- **Offprints** can be ordered by the corresponding author.
- **Color illustrations:** free online; print color incurs extra cost.
- **Proof reading:** check typesetting/conversion errors and completeness/accuracy; substantial content changes need Editor approval. Post-publication changes only via Erratum.
- **Online First:** published online after corrected proofs — the official, DOI-citable first publication.

## Open Choice & Open access publishing
Open Choice allows open-access publication in 1850+ Springer Nature journals (APCs vary by journal). Benefits: greater engagement, higher visibility/impact, easy funder-mandate compliance. Open Choice articles retain author copyright under a Creative Commons licence. See the journal's Open access publishing page for fees, funding, and licences.

# 한글 번역 — 공식 투고규정

## 저자 안내 (Instructions for Authors)

### 언어
영어가 모국어가 아닌 경우, 심사 대상이 되려면 원고를 충분히 교정하고 국제적 표현으로 다듬으며 RISE에 게재된 선행 연구와 대화해야 한다.

### 편집 절차 — 이중 익명 심사
본 학술지는 **이중 익명(double-blind)** 심사를 따른다. 심사 내내 저자는 심사자에게 익명으로 유지되며, 원고와 부속 자료의 익명화는 저자 책임이다.
- 저자명·소속, 그 밖에 신원을 드러낼 수 있는 모든 정보를 본문과 모든 첨부 파일(그림·보충자료 포함)에서 제거해야 한다.
- **별도의 표지(Title Page)**를 제출하며, 제목·저자명·소속·교신저자 연락처를 담는다. 감사의 글·이해관계 공개·연구비 정보도 이 표지에 포함한다.
- 저자는 자기 신원이 드러날 수 있는 방식의 자기인용을 피해야 한다.

### 논문 분량
**참고문헌 포함 최대 8,000단어**를 권장한다(더 짧아도 환영). 과학교육 지식에 "매우 중대한 독창적 기여"임을 설득력 있게 제시하는 드문 경우, 사전에 편집자에게 연락하면 장문 게재가 허용될 수 있다.

### 원고 제출
원고 제출은 다음을 의미한다: 해당 연구가 이전에 출판된 적 없음, 다른 곳에서 출판 심사 중이 아님, 공저자 전원과 연구 수행 기관의 책임 당국이 (암묵적·명시적으로) 출판을 승인했음. 보상 청구가 발생해도 출판사는 법적 책임을 지지 않는다.
- **허가(Permissions):** 이미 출판된 그림·표·본문을 포함하려면 인쇄·온라인 양쪽에 대해 저작권자의 허가를 받고 증빙을 함께 제출해야 한다.
- **온라인 제출:** "Submit manuscript" 링크를 따라 화면 안내대로 모든 파일을 업로드한다.
- **소스 파일:** 제출·수정 시마다 편집 가능한 소스 파일 전체를 제공해야 한다. 누락 시 심사 대상에서 제외된다. 본문은 **.docx 또는 LaTeX** 같은 일반 워드 형식으로 제출한다.

### 심사 절차
RISE 심사는 두 단계다.
- **1단계:** 공동 편집장이 초기 심사로 분량·구조·연구접근의 질·글쓰기 질·영어 능숙도·과학교육 연구 기여 가능성을 검토하여 ① 심사 없이 게재불가, ② 동료심사 회부(2단계), ③ 심사 전 수정 기회(예: 분량 축소) 중 하나로 결정한다. 통상 약 1주 소요.
- **2단계:** 공동편집자 또는 부편집자가 관리하며 RISE 심사위원 풀에서 2인이 배정된다. 이중 익명. 순조로우면 제출 후 **약 3개월 이내** 결과 통지. 대부분 수정이 요구되며 여러 차례 진행되기도 한다. 수정 응답 시 ① 심사 의견 반영(또는 미반영 사유) 정리 문서, ② 변경/추가 부분을 명확히 표시(예: 하이라이트)할 것을 요구한다.
- **5가지 결정 기준:** ① 경미한 수정, ② 수정, ③ 대폭 수정, ④ 게재불가 후 재투고, ⑤ 게재불가.
- **심사자 되기:** 보통 상위 학위 소지자(또는 지도교수 추천이 있는 박사후보 말기), 과학교육 분야 연구 활동, 심사 경험을 갖춘다. 통상 1주 내 수락/거절, 4주 내 심사 완료. 관심 시 공동 편집장(Kok-Sing Tang, James Davis, info@asera.org.au)에게 연락.

### 표지 (Title Page)
다음을 포함한다.
- **제목:** 간결하고 정보가 담길 것.
- **저자 정보:** 성명; 소속(기관·학과·도시·주·국가); 교신저자의 명확한 표시와 유효 이메일; 가능하면 16자리 ORCID.
- **대규모 언어모형(LLM):** ChatGPT 등 LLM은 저자 기준을 충족하지 못한다(저자됨에는 책임이 따르며 이는 LLM에 적용 불가). **LLM 사용은 Methods(연구 방법) 절에 적절히 기록**해야 한다. 사람이 작성한 글의 가독성·문체·문법·맞춤법·어조 개선 수준의 "AI 보조 교정"(생성적 편집·자율적 콘텐츠 생성 제외)은 **선언 불필요**하나, 최종 텍스트에 대한 사람의 책임이 요구된다.
- **초록:** **150–250단어**, 정의되지 않은 약어·출처 불명 참고 금지.
- **키워드:** 색인용 **4–6개**.

**Statements and Declarations(선언 절).** 게재 논문에 포함되도록 **"Statements and Declarations"** 제목 아래 아래 선언을 넣는다. *관련 선언이 누락된 원고는 미완성으로 반려된다.*
- **이해상충(Competing Interests):** 제출 연구와 직간접 관련된 재정적·비재정적 이해관계를 공개해야 한다(아래 "이해상충" 참조).

### 본문 (Text)
- **본문 형식:** Word로 제출. 표준 평문 글꼴(예: 10pt Times Roman), 강조는 이탤릭, 자동 페이지 번호 사용, 필드 기능 금지, 들여쓰기는 탭(스페이스바 금지), 표는 표 기능(스프레드시트 금지), 수식은 수식 편집기/MathType, .docx(Word 2007+) 또는 .doc 저장. 수식이 많으면 LaTeX 가능(Springer Nature LaTeX 템플릿 권장).
- **제목 수준:** 표시 제목은 **3단계 이하**.
- **약어:** 첫 등장 시 정의하고 이후 일관되게 사용.
- **각주:** 참고문헌 인용을 포함한 부가 정보 가능하나 인용만으로 구성하거나 서지정보 전체를 담으면 안 되며, 그림·표 불가. 본문 각주는 연속 번호, 표 각주는 위첨자 소문자(유의수준 등은 별표). 제목/저자 각주는 기호 없음. 미주가 아닌 각주 사용.
- **감사의 글:** 사람·연구비·기금 등에 대한 감사는 **표지의 별도 절**에 둔다. 연구비 지원 기관명은 전체 표기.

### 참고문헌 (References)
- **인용:** 본문에서 (성, 연도)로 인용한다. 참고문헌 목록 저자 수는 **APA 7판**을 따른다(저자 20명까지 전원 표기; 21명 이상이면 앞 19명 + 줄임표 + 마지막 저자). "et al."로 축약하면 그대로 유지된다.
- **참고문헌 목록:** 본문에 인용되고 출판(또는 게재 확정)된 문헌만 포함(개인 교신·미출판은 본문에만). 제1저자 성 기준 알파벳 정렬. 학술지명·도서명은 이탤릭. 가능하면 **DOI를 전체 링크 형태**로 기재("https://doi.org/abc"). (APA 7 예시는 영문 원문 참조: 학술지 논문·도서·도서 章·온라인 문서.)

### 비영어 제목의 서지정보
비영어 제목은 번역하여 원제 뒤 괄호에 병기한다. 예: *Raae, P. H. (2012). … (The Nordic educational model and the Danish upper secondary school). Nordic Studies in Education, 32(03–04), 311–320.*

### 표 (Tables)
모든 표는 아라비아 숫자로 번호를 매기고 본문에서 연속 순서로 인용하며, 구성 요소를 설명하는 캡션(제목)을 단다. 기출판 자료는 캡션 끝에 출처를 표기. 표 각주는 본문 아래에 위첨자 소문자(유의수준 등은 별표).

### 그림·삽화 지침
- **전자 제출:** 모든 그림을 전자 제출, 사용 그래픽 프로그램 표시. 선호 형식: **EPS**(벡터), **TIFF**(하프톤); MS Office 파일도 가능. 벡터 그래픽은 글꼴 임베드. 파일명은 `Fig`+번호(예: `Fig1.eps`).
- **선화(Line Art):** 음영 없는 흑백, 선 ≥ **0.1 mm(0.3 pt)**, 스캔/비트맵 선화 ≥ **1200 dpi**.
- **하프톤:** 사진·정교한 음영 그림, ≥ **300 dpi**, 확대 시 스케일바.
- **혼합(Combination):** 하프톤+선화, ≥ **600 dpi**.
- **컬러:** 온라인은 무료; 인쇄가 흑백이면 주요 정보가 흑백에서도 구별되게 하고 캡션에 색 언급 금지; RGB(채널당 8비트) 제출.
- **레터링:** Helvetica/Arial, 약 2–3 mm(8–12 pt) 일관 크기; 음영/외곽선 글자 회피; 삽화 안에 제목/캡션 금지.
- **그림 번호:** 아라비아 숫자, 연속 순서 인용, 부분은 (a)(b)(c); 부록 그림은 본문 번호를 이어감; 온라인 부록(SI) 그림은 별도 번호.
- **그림 캡션:** 간결하게, 그림 파일이 아닌 본문 파일에; **Fig.**(굵게)+번호(굵게)로 시작; 번호 뒤·캡션 끝에 구두점 없음; 기출판 자료는 캡션 끝에 출처.
- **배치·크기:** 본문 내에; 단 너비에 맞춤; 대형 학술지 84 mm(2단)/174 mm(1단), 높이 ≤ 234 mm; 소형 학술지 119 mm, 높이 ≤ 195 mm.
- **접근성:** 설명형 캡션; 색과 함께 패턴 사용; 글자 대비 ≥ 4.5:1.
- **생성형 AI 이미지:** Springer 생성형 AI 이미지 정책 준수.
