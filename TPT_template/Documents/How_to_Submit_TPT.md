How to Submit

Before submission, please review the instructions for Preparing Your Manuscript.

For additional information, please review the instructions for Web Posting Guidelines and Reprints.

New Manuscript Submissions

For initial submission, we recommend that authors upload a single PDF file that contains the manuscript text, figures, and any additional material necessary for a thorough review of your manuscript. A cover letter, supplementary video files, and any related manuscript files can be uploaded separately. Authors may submit separate source files for their manuscript text and figures, if they wish. After uploading files, authors will be required to input a few additional manuscript items, such as the title, abstract, and author information, as well as acknowledge policies to ensure that the submission complies with AIP Publishing policies.

To begin an initial submission, click “Submit Manuscript” and then select the appropriate manuscript type. After selecting the manuscript type, you will first be prompted to upload all manuscript files you would like considered as part of the submission. Follow the on-screen instructions to provide additional manuscript details. You will have the opportunity to review all submitted files before finalizing your submission. You may pause and save your submission at any stage and return to complete it later.

PLEASE NOTE: Required information is indicated with a red asterisk (*). Until you complete all the required steps and click “Submit Manuscript”, your manuscript is not considered as submitted.

 

Revised Manuscript Submissions

When submitting a revised manuscript, Authors must provide a point-by-point response to the reviewers’ comments and production-ready files. The manuscript text file should be submitted in Word format. Only one manuscript file may be submitted. Figures should be uploaded separately and submitted in PS, EPS, or TIFF format. Supplementary Material should be separated in a similar fashion to the main manuscript files and will be identified as Supplementary Material using the File Type and File Description fields within the submission process. Please note that manuscript data and files from the initial submission will be automatically carried over to the revision record.

As with new manuscripts, the submission of your revision is not complete until you have corrected any errors that are highlighted and/or marked with a large red arrow Red Attention Arrow and you have pressed the button to complete the submission.

 

Navigation and Getting Help

Throughout the submission process, red arrows Red Attention Arrow will indicate pending action items that you must address prior to submitting your manuscript. You will be able to navigate among the various screens during the submission process. If you navigate away from a screen without performing a required action or answering a required question, that screen will be designated as not complete with a red Red X; successfully completed screens will be designated with a green Green Check.

If you need additional help, you can click the ‘Help/Feedback’ link at the upper right corner of the page to submit a Peer X-Press Support request form to request additional assistance. Please include the following information in your message:

The name of the Journal and the assigned tracking number, if available.
Your full name
Your contact information, including e-mail address and telephone number
A description of problem encountered, with screenshots, if possible.
 

Checking the Status of Your Manuscript

After you have approved the file upload and conversion for your manuscript submission, you may obtain the status of your manuscript at any time by:

Logging in to the system with your user name and password.
Clicking on the “Live Manuscript” link under “Author Tasks” on your Home screen (main page).
Clicking on the manuscript number.
Clicking the “Check Status” link at the bottom of the screen.
You may also contact the Editorial Office directly by clicking the “Send Manuscript Correspondence” link.
